FADE IN:

INT. COFFEE SHOP - MORNING

SARAH, a young woman in her twenties, sits at a table with her laptop. She looks up as JESSICA approaches.

JESSICA
Hey Sarah! How's the job search going?

SARAH
It's been tough, Jessica. I've had three interviews this week, but I'm not sure any of them are the right fit.

JESSICA
What kind of work are you looking for?

SARAH
I really want to get into marketing. I have my degree in communications, but most entry-level positions require two years of experience.

JESSICA
That's the classic catch-22. Have you considered internships?

SARAH
I've applied to a few, but they're mostly unpaid. I need something that pays the bills.

JESSICA
I hear you. The economy is tough right now. But don't give up - something will come through.

SARAH
Thanks, Jessica. I appreciate the encouragement.

JESSICA
Of course! That's what friends are for. Want to grab lunch later?

SARAH
That sounds great. I could use a break from all this job searching.

JESSICA
Perfect! I know a great place around the corner.

SARAH
Excellent. I'll see you at noon then.

JESSICA
See you then!

SARAH smiles and goes back to her laptop as JESSICA walks away.

FADE OUT.
